How to Register

4 Easy Steps to Ship Your Goods

Once your supplier delivers to our China warehouse, follow these steps to get your cargo moving to Uganda.

1

Deliver to Our China Warehouse

Tell your supplier to ship to our Guangzhou warehouse address above and obtain a delivery note / receipt.

2

Fill in This Registration Form

Provide your contact details, cargo description, weight, and choose your preferred shipping method.

3

Upload Your Delivery Note

Attach the proof of delivery / delivery note from your supplier as confirmation your goods arrived at our warehouse.

4

We Confirm & Ship

Our team reviews your registration, confirms cargo receipt, and arranges shipment to Uganda. We'll keep you updated throughout.

📌 Important Note

Always mark your packages clearly with your Full Name and Phone Number so our China team can identify your goods on arrival. Unidentified packages may be delayed.
