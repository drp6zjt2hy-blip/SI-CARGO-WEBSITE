Our Story

Built for Ugandan Importers

SI CARGO UGANDA was founded with a single purpose: to make importing from China simple, affordable, and stress-free for Ugandan businesses and individuals. We noticed that many Ugandans faced challenges with unreliable freight agents, hidden charges, and cargo delays — and we decided to fix that.

Today, we are one of Uganda's growing cargo specialists, operating a warehouse in China and a delivery hub in Kampala. From single packages to full container loads, we handle it all.

  • Licensed customs clearing agents in Uganda
  • Bonded warehouse facility in China
  • Transparent, all-inclusive pricing
  • Dedicated account managers for each client
  • WhatsApp & phone support 6 days a week
